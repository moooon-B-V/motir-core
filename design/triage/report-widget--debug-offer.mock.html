Report widget — the Debug with Motir AI offer

MOTIR-7045 · story MOTIR-7042. A person who has just reported a bug in the header Report widget is offered Debug with Motir AI. Pressing it closes the widget, opens the one Motir AI surface on the project conversation, and sends the debug turn straight away. The seed is the person's own title and description, and the turn is anchored on the triage bug they just filed.

Why the widget stays open

Today a 201 closes the widget and fires a Toast. A Toast dismisses itself after 5 s and holds one short action label. That is too little time and too little room for a control that starts paid work. So when the offer applies, the widget stays open and its body becomes this success state. The same two strings the Toast uses (triage.widget.submitted and submittedDetail, both unchanged) head it, so no toast fires.

The press is the go (A1.3 condition 2). The helper line sits right above the button and is its aria-describedby. It says, before the press, that pressing starts the debug and spends credits. The label is a verb phrase, not a navigation.

What the press does. The widget closes, then the Motir AI overlay opens on the project conversation (useOpenPlanningWorkspace, project context, the orb's href). It sends ONE turn through the shipped autoSendTurn seam, claimed once under autoSendKey so a remount cannot send it twice. The turn is { body, targets: [PROD-412] } and carries no intent (§1). The body is the person's title, a blank line, then their description, word for word. With no description, it is the title alone (A1.3 condition 1).

isMotirAiConfigured() is false (a self-hosted install with no Motir AI). Same result as (a): the shipped close + Toast, byte for byte. The gate is the one the layout already computes for the orb (showPlanWithAi), handed to ReportProvider beside canEdit.

c · a Feature, not a Bug

Thanks — your report was submitted Filed as PROD-413, now waiting in Requested features.

The offer is for the Bug kind only. A Feature submission (kind task) closes and toasts as it does today, whatever the actor's rights.

One rule covers all three: the success panel exists only to carry the offer. When the offer does not apply, the panel is not rendered and the shipped behaviour is the whole behaviour. A disabled Debug with Motir AI would promise something the person cannot do. It would cost a tab stop and a screen-reader announcement, and it would push them to spend credits they may not control. The orb registry says the same about a dead row (lib/planning/aiCallout.ts).

Out of credits is not in this panel. The offer is still rendered when the organization has no credits left. The press sends the turn, and the rail shows the shipped paywall in its own slot (debug-turn.mock.html panel 5), which is where the owner and member faces of that answer already live.
