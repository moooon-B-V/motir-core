The planning surface's left pane holds the plan page's List | Canvas

MOTIR-6184 · story MOTIR-6155. A DELTA mock: only the panels that change. It amends the planning surface's left pane for a proposed or decided plan, and nothing else.

It COMPOSES, and does not redraw: the pane header and its Segmented switch (Part VIII §2), PlanProposalList (Part VIII §3, Part XIII §7), PlanReviewCanvas (Part IX, Part XIII), and PlanChangeConfirmBar with its decline band, held reason and stale alert — the approved decide design of MOTIR-6033, evidence cmueg6bue00kghwoikl5e6bxp, §20.4 and §20.5. (That design's note section is published as "Part XX" and is not in the committed design-notes.md, whose Part XX is MOTIR-6134's difficulty design — so it is cited here by card key and evidence id, never by number. Bug MOTIR-6190.)

Every pane header, list row, confirm bar and decline band below is the shipped markup, rendered from the real components through renderWithIntl on origin/main @ d0976c0ab — not a hand-drawn stand-in. The Close + project bar and the resting footer are copied verbatim from PlanningWorkspaceHost.tsx at that commit. The plan-review canvas is not redrawn: it is a labelled region, because nothing inside it changes — the same treatment MOTIR-6033's own asset gives it. The first stylesheet is the project's real Tailwind v4 output, compiled over this document's class attributes; colour is --el-* only and shape is the element-semantic tokens.

Sheet 1 — where the switch sits, and when it appears at allThe surface already has a top bar the plan page does not: Close · Esc · the project name, the overlay's own chrome. The component's 44px pane header sits beneath it, as a second bar, and it appears only once a plan is PROPOSED. With no plan there is no switch and the pane is PlanChangeCanvas exactly as shipped — so the resting surface is unchanged and the 45px is paid only while there is a plan to read.
