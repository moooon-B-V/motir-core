A proposal FILED into a folder, on the plan review

How the plan review tells a reviewer that a proposal will be filed into a folder rather than hung under a work item — on the canvas, in the list, under Show changes, when the folder is gone, and when its path is long. Every card, row, crumb and rail below is the SHIPPED component’s markup (PlanItemNode, PlanProposalList, PlanReviewRail, ProjectRoadmapCanvas’s chrome), dumped through the shipped test harness; the NEW elements are the placement line, the folder crumb segment, the folder side of a diff, the list’s folder fact and the deleted-folder state.

Reached exactly as every plan is: Plans in the left nav → a plan row, or the item page’s pending-plan indicator → the plan. This Part adds no entrance. The folder vocabulary — the folder glyph and the Parked ▸ 2025 path — is design/work-items/folders.mock.html’s and design/work-items/placement.mock.html’s, composed and not redrawn.

PANEL 1 — An add filed into a folder, at arrivalA plan proposes one story filed into Parked ▸ 2025 and two subtasks under it. 1a: the canvas ARRIVES where the plan most fills — the proposed story's level (Part IX §1, unchanged) — and the breadcrumb gains ONE folder segment ahead of the proposed crumb: text with the folder glyph, not a button, because no canvas level is a folder. 1b: Back to the top level. The filed story is a ROOT, so it sits among the roots; its folder is the line under its title, in the slot a modify spends on its diff. Show changes is armed on arrival exactly as shipped. A reviewer without work_item:edit sees the same pixels — nothing here is an action.

PANEL 4 — The stale folder: deleted after the plan was writtenThe folder a proposal names no longer exists, so its NAME no longer exists either — the card cannot say Parked ▸ 2025 and does not pretend to. While planned the card wears the SHIPPED Out of date badge with a new reason, and its placement line becomes Folder deleted behind the folder-x glyph — words first, never colour alone. The rail's shipped stale summary lists it, and Approve is disabled with a hint naming the two real exits: a refusal nobody can clear by retrying is a dead control. 4b: the folder was deleted AFTER the page loaded — Approve was live, the server refused the whole approve, and the rail says so in its shipped role="alert" slot, naming the PROPOSAL (the folder has no name left to name); the refetch then puts the card and hint into 4a's state. Nothing was created and the plan stays Ready to review.
