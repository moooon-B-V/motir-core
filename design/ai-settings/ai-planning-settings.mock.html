Panel 6 — the pause, made legible (amendment · MOTIR-1739). MOTIR-916's cadence trigger SKIPS a project whose plan is still undecided, and nothing expires a plan — so the silence can last forever with no signal anywhere. This state says it out loud on the page where auto-plan is configured. It is the SAME .callout box the guardrail uses, in the .gate role (--el-tint-peach + --el-warning) this asset already defines for "the setting is on, but the feature is not running — here is why"; paused and not-connected are the same message family and cannot co-occur, so the role is REUSED rather than a fourth tint invented. The link is the way out — it goes to the shipped plan detail (/plans/[id], MOTIR-847), reached today only from the Plans list; on a tint it takes --el-text-strong + an underline, never --el-link (4.13:1 on peach — under AA). The out-of-date face carries the SHIPPED stale badge — --el-tint-yellow + --el-text-strong + TriangleAlert, markup copied from components/planning/PlanItemNode.tsx, plus a hairline so it reads on the peach — and names the drift in the app's own words ("may be out of date", aiPlanning.mayBeOutOfDate). Pausing is not disabling: the switch and the threshold stepper stay fully interactive in both paused faces (compare panel 5, where a non-admin's controls really are disabled) — the user can still reconfigure while a plan waits. Nothing paused renders when auto-plan is off, or when no plan is undecided: that is the left-hand card, unchanged from panel 2.

What the promise may and may not claim
The first sentence is a COMMITMENT and Motir's own to make. Neither Motir nor its gateway trains on the content — that is a fact about our own systems, it is stated in the same words on the public provider page, and it is the half a reader is entitled to hold us to.
The second is a REPORT, and it must not read as a promise. What a provider does is that provider's published position, not something Motir can undertake on its behalf — which is exactly why the sentence hands the reader to the page that carries each provider's own answer rather than summarising it here.
No provider fact appears in this component. No retention window, no "trains: yes", no provider names. Those live in the gateway (motir/datapolicy) and on the provider page; a third copy in a React component is how this data has already gone stale four times.
No non-admin panel is drawn, deliberately. This is read-only copy — it renders identically for a member who cannot edit the page, so a second panel would differ from this one by nothing. The lock banner and the disabled controls above it are panel 5's and are unchanged.

Panel 7 — the whole amendment. A callout in the neutral role at the foot of the EXISTING Planner card body, below the model picker: no new card, no new control, no new primitive and no new CSS. The link reuses the asset's own .clink grammar, which takes --el-text-strong + an underline rather than --el-link — a rule §9 already carries, for the AA reason recorded there. Placement is the argument: the promise sits where the model is chosen, because choosing the model is the act it qualifies. Higher up the page it would read as a claim about auto-plan; on its own card it would read as a control. ⚠️ The link target is motir-marketing's since MOTIR-4103, not a route this repository serves — the mock draws the destination, and §D3 records that the implementing card resolves the href through motir-core's existing public-site origin rather than hard-coding a host.
