Organization administration

Story 6.10 · Subtask 6.10.1 design asset — built from app/globals.css tokens. The Organization is the new TOP tenancy tier above the workspace (the root account, parent of N workspaces, and the billing entity). Surfaces: the org switcher in the shell, org settings, and cross-workspace member management. Mirror = Atlassian org admin / Linear workspace-Owner settings (rung 1). Billing/credit is 7.12.5 / Epic 8 and ABSENT here; the cross-org platform console is Epic 10. Source of truth = this HTML; see design-notes.md. Gates 6.10.5.

Workflows & statuses, custom fields, labels, components, automation and dashboards are workspace-scoped (they live on the Workspace row underneath — there is NO org→workspace config inheritance in the data). With one workspace they appear in this single Settings home and save to that workspace. These sections are owned by their own design areas and are not redrawn here.

⚠️ CORRECTED. This arm first drew Git as absent for a plain member. That was wrong, and wrong in a way §6 of docs/decisions/organization-tier.md names outright: “a hidden tier may not remove a capability … relocating a surface preserves its gate.” The surface this row relocates FROM, /settings/workspace/github, checks a session and a workspace context and no role at all — every workspace member reads it today, and every workspace member is an org member by §5’s upward invariant. Admin-gating the row would have taken that away silently. The owner/admin gate belongs on the page’s WRITE controls (Connect · Disconnect · Remove), which is where panel 7c puts it.

What survives from the first drawing is the disposition, unchanged and still right for the rows it applies to: absent, not disabled — an entry point is a promise about a room. It is what Billing does off cloud and Security does below the workspace-tier reveal: the row is not rendered and the rows below close up, with no disabled row and no tooltip.

Where it goes, and why there. The shipped menu’s own reasoning for Security is that it sits directly under Settings because it is a settings-shaped destination and keeping it above Members holds the two account-level concerns together. Git is the third such concern — the organisation’s own resources — so it joins that block rather than landing among the people and money rows. The menu then reads configuration → people → money, which is the order it already had.

⚠️ SUPERSEDED BY PANEL 7 — kept verbatim as the reading that produced it. This note read: “There is no org settings RAIL to put a row in. /settings/organization/* has no area layout and no rail — unlike settings/project/ and settings/account/, each of which has one. This menu, plus the command palette, IS the organisation’s settings navigation, which is why the row lands here and why MOTIR-4672’s Panel 6 draws its page with no rail beside it.”

Every FACT in it is still true of the shipped tree; the inference was not. A tier that is the only one of three without a settings nav is a gap, and answering “where does the Git row go” with “the pop-over, because that is all there is” designs the gap in permanently — a pop-over you must already know to open, that closes behind you and highlights nothing. Panel 7 builds the rail. This menu keeps its row as the fast door beside it, and MOTIR-4672’s panel 6 gains the rail its page was drawn without.

⚠️ THIS PANEL REPLACES THE ANSWER PANEL 6 GAVE ON ITS OWN, AND SUPERSEDES ITS CLOSING NOTE. That note read “there is no org settings RAIL to put a row in … this menu, plus the command palette, IS the organisation’s settings navigation.” It was an accurate reading of the tree and the wrong thing to design against: it took a GAP for a convention. The org menu is a pop-over you must already know to open, it closes behind you, and it highlights nothing — so a person who arrives at /settings/organization from the rail’s bottom Settings row (which is where that row points with no active project) sees one page of cards and no way onward. Panel 6’s row is kept: it is the FAST door, and it is how the two other sub-pages have always been reached. This panel adds the DURABLE one.

Nothing moves. Every existing page keeps its route and its content — the four sub-routes (members · security · usage · billing) become rows, the index becomes the Organisation row, and Git is the one NEW row. In particular WorkspaceFoldInSection stays exactly where it is, on the index page: below the workspace-tier reveal that page hosts two tiers’ sections, and relocating them is a §6 decision this card has no business re-taking.
