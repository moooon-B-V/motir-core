MOTIR-5727 draws what a work item linked to monitor issues shows, the door a person uses to link an existing work item to an issue by hand, and the way to take the link back. It is the Development section’s card grammar with a different source.

Panel 1 · One linked error — and the ACCESS PATH: opening a work item, the late upper tier, directly below Development

The ordinary monitor-filed bug. The row is the shipped PullRequestRow grammar with a different source: title (links out to the issue in Sentry), the connection plus environment · release on the identifier line, then the level pill, Seen N times and last seen (absolute time on hover). No provider call: every fact is the stored monitor_issue row.
