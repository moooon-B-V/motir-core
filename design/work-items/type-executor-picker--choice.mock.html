Notes

Order. The ADR's §1e list: … decision · choice · deploy · manual · legal · chore. The group stays one contiguous run, so the menu's section heading and WORK_ITEM_TYPE_GROUP need no reshuffle.

Glyph. lucide signpost — a fork in the road, one sign pointing each way. It is deliberately NOT a variant of decision's scale: the two share a hue, so the glyph is what tells them apart.

Hue. --el-type-choice: var(--color-charcoal), declared beside --el-type-decision in packages/design-system/theme.css's [data-appearance-scope] block. The palette's seven saturated hues and three greys are spent; the precedent (legal joins decision, copy/translate join content) is that a newcomer takes its nearest neighbour's hue and its OWN token. Hue names the family; the glyph names the member.
