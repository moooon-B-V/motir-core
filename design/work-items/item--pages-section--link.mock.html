22Keyboard and assistive technology

NOTES. Behaviour of the panels above, in the shipped live-region pattern.

behaviour · light

  • Door. Focus moves into the search field when the door opens and returns to the door when the form closes.
  • Separate stops. A row’s page link and its Unlink are two tab stops with two names: the page’s title, and Unlink page {title}.
  • Announcements. Just linked, Unlinked “{title}”. and each refusal are announced once through the live region; the pinned row is announced as it arrives.
  • Unavailable row. The title is not a tab stop — there is nothing to open — so the row contributes exactly one stop, its Unlink, named Unlink unavailable page where the row has no title.
  • Keys. Enter picks the highlighted candidate; Escape closes the form and returns focus to the door; Show more keeps focus on itself as candidates append.
