Design result — what to review (a delta)

Story MOTIR-5488 · Subtask MOTIR-5494. Amends design/work-items/design-result.mock.html and design-notes.md § ⭐ Design result panel. A result is the changed mock(s) with the note one link away — no inline note, no screenshot strip. Only the panels that change are drawn; everything unlabelled is the shipped panel as it stands.

1 · A current result, one mock — the mock leads, the note is a LINK below it

The mock is what a reviewer is asked to judge, so it comes first and nothing sits above it. The note is for whoever wants more, so its link row sits at the bottom, under the frame. It opens the published note_file attachment in a new tab. The frame, its strip and the provenance line are the shipped ones, unchanged.
