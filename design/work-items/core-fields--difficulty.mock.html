The Difficulty field — item page + quick view

MOTIR-6097, for MOTIR-6101. A leaf (task · subtask · bug) carries a difficulty of low · medium · high, or none. It sits directly below Executor on both surfaces. The value is a plain label with a quiet signal glyph in the faint ink — never a coloured pill, so it cannot be mistaken for Priority (which shares the words Medium and High). The dashed outline marks the new element; it is review chrome, not part of the design.
