The DIFFICULTY editor at four segments

MOTIR-6200, for MOTIR-6199. A delta on core-fields--difficulty.mock.html, whose editor panels draw three segments — Low · Medium · High. The scale gained a fourth level, Trivial, after that design was published, and the four-segment control is wider than the rail it lives in on both surfaces that draw it.

The decision this delta records: the editor's track fills its rail and divides it evenly, and the leading glyph is dropped in the editor only. Read mode is unchanged. Nothing else about the field moves.

The dashed red outline marks the defect; the dashed violet outline marks what changes. Both are review chrome, not part of the design.

1 · The defect — today, at the item page's 18rem rail

As shipped. The track cannot wrap, shrink or scroll, so it paints over the card's right border and past the rail. High is cut off at the panel edge. Clear wraps correctly — the picker's wrapper is flex-wrap — but a flex wrapper cannot split the track.

Difficulty

The card is clipped here so the overflow is visible as a reader meets it.

5 · Why fill, and not the two alternatives

5 · Dropping the glyph ALONE leaves the width CONTENT-SIZED — it is the cheapest fix, and it keeps the property that caused the defect: the track is still exactly as wide as its labels and its token spacing make it. Here it is drawn with the glyph removed under soft-playful's 14px control spacing, the widest case. Read the measurement below in your own browser — whichever way it lands for your font, it is a fit that has to be re-argued for every style, every locale and every future level, which is what ruled it out.

Difficulty

What the reader is looking at. Every panel above is live markup carrying the shipped component's own geometry, and the figures under each panel are measured by your browser as you read them — not transcribed. A panel that says FITS has been measured fitting in the viewer you are holding.

Why the numbers are measured rather than written down. The control's width depends on the reader's font, and the app ships a system font stack. A fix that fits by a few pixels on one platform is a fix that overflows on another — which is the second reason the fill variant wins: its width is the rail's, in every font, at every style, and at a fifth level.
