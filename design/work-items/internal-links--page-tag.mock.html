Roadmap principles
The section order is fixed: People, Work items, Pages. People keeps its shipped rule (the header shows only when a member matches). ↑/↓ wraps across all three sections and Enter inserts the active row. A page row is a FileText glyph in --el-text-secondary, the title in --el-text (one line, truncated), and its place on the right in --el-text-secondary. The place is the folder path joined with › or, for a child page, its parent page’s title. A page at the top level shows no place. The active row is --el-surface, which is why the place takes secondary and not muted.

7 · Page unavailable — one state for three causes
Read view and editor alike
Scope is in Page unavailable.
The reader has no page:view, the page is archived, or the page is deleted. All three draw this chip, and nothing tells them apart. The title does not appear anywhere: not in the text, not in a title attribute, not in hidden text. A Lock glyph and “Page unavailable” in --el-text-secondary on --el-muted, same badge shape. It is a span, not a link: no hover, no focus stop, default cursor.
