Motir Search — the fourth billed line (MOTIR-4551 · amendment to 8.1.3)

An amendment to design/billing/billing.mock.html, following the structural precedent ci-line.mock.html set: a billed line that arrived after the panel shipped gets its own asset, never a re-export of a frozen mock. It adds ONE thing — the “Motir Search” line. Its EXISTENCE is already decided, in the same words, by motir-gateway/docs/decisions/motir-search-channel.md §4.4 and motir-ai/docs/credit-model.md §4b (“its own kind, alongside AI turns and Motir CI. It is not merged into the AI line”); what this asset decides is what it LOOKS LIKE, which nothing had drawn. ①②③, the payment card, the storefront and the access path are reproduced VERBATIM where shown and are unchanged. Every figure is a named field of BillingStatusDTO.search (MOTIR-4555); nothing is drawn that no read supplies. Toggle dark mode to verify --el-* token parity.

1 · IN PLACE — the whole billing panel at real width. The amendment’s only structural change: a FOURTH card between ③ Motir CI and Payment & invoices. ①, ② and ③ are copied verbatim from the base assets and are untouched — shown so the new line is reviewed in its real neighbourhood and at its real width, never as a floating fragment.
