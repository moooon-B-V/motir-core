Billing & plans, delta — the Calculator takes model categories and offers a larger pool (MOTIR-8636)

Amends billing--calculator.mock.html: the per-lane model pickers are gone. The input is one set of one to three categories, each listing its models as examples only; the estimate is shared equally across them and the answer prints the split; a high end past the largest regular pool is answered with a larger pool plan from the answer, not a stack of top-ups. Fourteen panels: S2, S3, S5, S6, S7, S8, S9, S10, S11, S12, S13, S14, zh, narrow. Every figure is illustrative and carries its source on the panel; the mock does no share arithmetic. Toggle dark to check --el-* parity.
