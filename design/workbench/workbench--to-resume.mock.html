To resume: the Workbench tab for runs waiting at a gate (MOTIR-7702)

Design asset for MOTIR-7702 (Story MOTIR-7701). A DELTA on workbench.mock.html's strip and landing cascade (§ 21 / § 30) that COMPOSES To fix's per-run entry (§ 34). A parent run that stops because its remaining work waits on an approval closes gated, not dead; its work items leave In progress and To fix and wait here, ONE entry per run, until the gate is decided. Spec: design-notes.md § 35. The code is MOTIR-7712 (this tab) on MOTIR-7707's column.

Panel 0 — the strip gains To resume, third, with a count; the cascade gains the same rung
Waiting on you1 To fix1 To resume3 In progress2 To do7 Recently finished2 Watching5

The landing cascade — amended

  1. Waiting on you if anything awaits the reader's decision
  2. else To fix if anything of theirs is stuck until repaired
  3. else To resume if a run of theirs stopped at a gate (new)
  4. else In progress if anything of theirs is moving
  5. else To do — terminal

Why third. A run waiting at a gate needs the reader LESS urgently than either rung above it: the decision itself is already on Waiting on you (it outranks, and that is where the reader acts), and a broken run on To fix will not move without a repair, while a gated run moves the moment the gate is approved. It needs the reader MORE than In progress, whose work moves without them: a terminal or runbook run that is ready to resume waits for motir continue, and a hosted one that could not resume waits for a person. So it sits between To fix and In progress, in the strip and in the cascade, with ONE order for both — the strip still reads as an explanation of where the reader landed.

The count is ENTRIES (runs), exactly as To fix counts since § 34 — COUNT(DISTINCT resumeRunId) over the reader's slice (MOTIR-7707 tabCounts).

The tab id is to-resume (?tab=to-resume, MOTIR-7707 adds it to WORKBENCH_TABS after to-fix). Its glyph is lucide circle-pause — a run that paused, not one that broke (To fix keeps wrench). Label To resume · zh 待继续. The badge is the shipped count chip. resolveWorkbenchLanding gains if (counts.toResume > 0) return 'to-resume' between the To fix and In progress rungs.
