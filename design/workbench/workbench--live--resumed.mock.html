On this tab nothing the reader was looking at moves, on either page. The list is the shipped pager’s 25-row pages (IssueListPager, its numbered nav and its previous / next steps), and the order is createdAt asc, so every arrival is the NEWEST row and lands at the end of the LAST page — here page 2, after the rows the reader held, each with its New chip. MOTIR-4942 and MOTIR-5008 were decided by somebody else while the tab was hidden: both are HELD in place with --el-text-secondary ink and the colourless Decided elsewhere pill, and both still open. Two numbers move, and they are different numbers. The strip counts what is AWAITING: 29, minus two held, plus three arrived, is 30. The pager counts the rows in the list, and a held row is still a row until the next load: 29 plus three is Showing 26–32 of 32. That is § 26’s rule — a held row is a receipt, not a member — applied to the two numbers on screen. A reader on page 1 sees no row change at all: its 25 rows are the oldest and the catch-up adds nothing to them, so only the strip count and the pager total move (of 29 → of 32). The page does not jump to where the arrivals landed, no chip points at page 2, and the page count grows only when the arrivals cross a 25-row boundary. No Reconnecting… chip sits beside the strip: the stream was closed on purpose, so its reopen is not a fault, and the rows the reader last saw keep full ink while the catch-up read is in flight.
