9 · Two intensities, one decision — light

Subtle (top) is recommended. The colourful variant adds no hue — it doubles the chip wash to 22% and tints the column counts by status, which changes component recipes the palette layer cannot own. Every hue it shows is the same as the recommendation; what it adds is recipe, not palette, so it belongs to a component card, not this one.
