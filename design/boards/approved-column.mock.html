Global minimum 11.6 (amber/dark vs planning); against done specifically the range is 14.2 – 42.6. Contrast is 9.54 – 13.72:1 on --el-card / --el-surface / --el-page-bg, against the 3:1 bar. It takes its own unspent violet, color-mix(in srgb, var(--color-accent) 40%, var(--color-ink)), declared in BOTH themes — the cascade-pairing rule that suite enforces — for 14.3 / 12.3.

And the chip is a different measurement from the dot — measured, with a control. The ΔE floor above is on the DOT, which paints the hue at full strength; a chip is that hue at 14% over the surface, so every chip in this product compresses into a narrow band. Approved's chip is nearest done's at ΔE2000 5.7 (sienna/light), ink 7.59 – 11.97:1 for --el-text-strong. That number means nothing without the control, so here it is: the chip set the product ALREADY ships has a global minimum of ΔE 2.8 (citrine/dark, planned vs in-progress). Approved's worst pair is twice as separated as the worst pair on the board today. This is why the glyph is load-bearing rather than decorative — at 14% no status is told apart by tint alone, which is the position StatusPill's own header already takes. Do not read the dot's ΔE 11.6 as a claim about the chip.

The glyph, because colour alone is forbidden (finding #35). approved carries CircleCheck and done keeps Check: the same mark, formalised — a person put a ring around it. It joins the Circle* vocabulary this ramp already speaks (CircleDot · CircleDotDashed · CircleEllipsis). In Review stays unglyphed, as it is today. Labels: en “Approved”, zh “已批准” — the string this product already ships for an approval in acceptance.status.approved and planReview.status_approved, not a new coinage.

The cardinality constraint this mock must not contradict: a project's status set is per-project and customisable, so nine columns is the DEFAULT workflow's shape and never a fixed count. And approved has no surface of its own — it adds no route, no nav entry and no affordance; it appears inside surfaces a person already reaches, namely design/boards/board.mock.html, design/work-items/ (the item page's status control) and design/roadmap/ (the canvas node).
