Set up a second factor to continue — the forced-enrolment screen

Story 8.13 (MOTIR-1215) · Subtask 8.13.2 (MOTIR-3643). The screen a member without a second factor meets once their organization or workspace starts requiring one. It is not an error state: nothing has gone wrong, nobody was removed, nothing was deleted, and there is no deadline. It asks for one thing, says who is asking, offers the three ways to satisfy it, and gives the person a way to leave.

1 · Held, mandated by the organization — the arrival
The default case. The copy names the ORGANISATION, states plainly that access resumes as soon as any second factor is set up, and says outright that nothing was lost. The destination chip is the thing that makes the promise concrete — it shows the person that Motir knows where they were going. Below it, the arrival drawn as a flow: nobody clicks to reach this screen.

3 · Held, mandated by both — the organization is named
⚠️ The rule: the HIGHEST mandating tier is the one named. When both require it, the organization is reported, because it is the floor — naming the workspace would suggest that leaving the workspace, or getting its policy switched off, would help. It would not. The body adds one sentence so the person is not misled about who to ask.

4 · Choosing a method — three routes, one honest trade-off each
Ordered by how fast the person can be done, not by how secure the method is: a passkey is both the fastest AND the strongest, so the two orderings agree at the top. Email codes are labelled the lower-security fallback exactly as the account Security pane already labels them — the honesty is the point, and it is what stops this reading as a demand.
M Motir

Choose how to sign in

Any one of these satisfies what Acme is asking for. You can add the others later.

Use a passkey Fastest

Your device's fingerprint, face or PIN. Nothing to install, nothing to type, and it cannot be phished.

Use an authenticator app

A six-digit code from an app like 1Password, Authy or Google Authenticator. Works with no signal.

Email me a code Least secure

We email a code each time you sign in. Nothing to set up — but anyone who reaches your inbox reaches your account.

7 · The way out — mandatory on every held panel
⚠️ A sign-out control is not optional here, and this panel exists to say why. Every other route in the product is closed to this person, so a screen with no exit is a trap: somebody on a borrowed laptop, or without their phone, or who simply does not want to do this right now, must be able to leave rather than be bounced between a redirect and a screen they cannot satisfy. It is a GHOST button in the footer — present on every held panel, never competing with the primary action — and the line under it removes the fear that leaving costs them something.
You can come back and set this up any time you sign in.
8 · Dark — the same screen, tokens flipped
Every element takes its colour from an --el-* token. The two chips carry their hue in the BACKGROUND with --el-text-strong ink, which is what keeps them AA in both themes, and no panel anywhere in this asset uses --el-danger.
