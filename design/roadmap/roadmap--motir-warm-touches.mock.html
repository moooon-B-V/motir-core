Warm touches in the default Motir palette — the roadmap

What the new colours add on /roadmap. In every pair the top frame is what ships today and the bottom one is the recommended proposal. A roadmap node draws a kind ICON, never a work-type chip (the Manual chip is the one exception), so the citrine Design hue does not reach the roadmap. What changes is the Epic glyph and the peach and yellow tints, and nothing else.

R1 · Root level — light

The roadmap's root is your epics, so the orange lands hardest here: every root node's Epic glyph turns from the link blue to the warm orange, on its rose tile (3.01:1, at the 3.0 floor; the tile is aria-hidden and the key and title carry the meaning). The Blocked chip (peach) and the Planning chip (yellow) warm a little. The ink You-are-here pill and border, the green progress meters, the mint Ready wash, the sky Done card and every edge are unchanged.
