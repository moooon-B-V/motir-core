The truncation tile. Last cell of the level. Not a door.

Grouping is not the cap fix, and must not be mistaken for one. The cap is applied by the level READ, before anything can be grouped — so collapsing the non-epic rows removes none of the pressure on it. The two halves of MOTIR-3490 are independent, and this tile is the second one. It reports a fact the reader currently cannot see at all: today the level simply stops at 200 rows, sorted by key ascending, so the rows it drops are the newest epics, with no cursor and no message.

The dashed edge is borrowed deliberately: the onboarding init screen's plan-preview cluster already ends its epic strip with a dashed + N more epics tile, so the canvas vocabulary for "there is more of this than is drawn" exists. The difference is the Show all action — that tile caps a preview on purpose, this one reports an overflow the reader must be able to escape.

A root of the SPRINT view, not of the tree: its parentId names an epic, and the sprint read re-rooted it because that epic is not a member. It is the sprint's actual work and must stay on the road.

A kind !== 'epic' rule alone would have swept nearly the whole sprint into one node. Sprint scope re-roots the level at the topmost in-sprint items, which are usually stories and subtasks — every one of them not an epic. The parentId === null conjunct is what keeps the two apart, and the wire already carries parentId, so nothing new is needed to tell them apart.
6 · Anatomy — what the grouped node borrows, and what it does not every slot accounted for
Slot On a work-item node On the grouped node
Box 280 × 124, --radius-card, --el-border, --el-surface, --shadow-card Identical. It is a node on the same board and must sit on the same grid.
Status pill The workflow status, its own label and category Empty. The group has no status. A "Mixed" or "7 open" chip in that slot would be a claim about work the node does not own.
Kind tile The kind's --el-type-* hue behind its glyph Neutral --el-muted behind a layers glyph in --el-text-secondary. No --el-type-* — no kind backs it, and borrowing one would say the group IS that kind.
Identifier MOTIR-<n>, mono, --el-text-secondary The count — 7 items — in the same slot and the same type. It is what identifies this node, and no key exists to put there.
Title The work item's title, two lines Not in an epic. See decision 1 for why not "Defects".
Drill chevron Present when the item has children Always present. The node exists only when it has members.
Progress meter The container's subtree done / total Absent. The meter means "this container's own work is N% done"; the group is a drawer, not a unit of work, and its members are unrelated to each other.
Quick-view viewable — select surfaces a View button Not viewable. Like the planning-origin card, its detail IS the level below it.
decorative Never Never. It holds real work, so it counts as a branch when the canvas asks whether a level offers a choice. See decision 3.
Two slots left deliberately empty is the point of this sheet. The status pill and the progress meter are the two places a synthetic node is most tempted to say something, and both would be saying it about work the node does not own. An empty slot reports that honestly; a filled one reports a number the reader will act on.
