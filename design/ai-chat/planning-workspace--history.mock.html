History header: one full-width row directly under the rail head and above the thread, present whenever the rail is (every mode, open or ended session). Collapsed on every overlay open; no count, no badge. Drawn with keyboard focus: it is a <button aria-expanded aria-controls>, reached by Tab after the head controls, toggled by Enter or Space. The chevron points right when collapsed and down when expanded.
