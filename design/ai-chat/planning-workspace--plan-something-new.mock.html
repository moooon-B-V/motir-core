Plan something new — the control, the confirm, and the new session opening in place

MOTIR-7647 · story MOTIR-7631. From inside the planning overlay a person can close the session they are in and start planning something else, by pressing Plan something new or by saying so. Either way Motir asks them to confirm first. On Confirm the session closes, its cards go back, and a new, empty session opens in the same overlay. Keep planning closes nothing.

The access path is the overlay of an open planning session, from any of its doors (the Plan door on a work item, the orb, a Plans row). Every panel is the rail at its default 480px unless it says otherwise.

The words are the person’s own turn, so they stay in the thread. The classifier read them as new_session; core wrote the confirm and ran no job.

The confirm is a planner bubble with the fixed question and two answers under it: Confirm (primary) and Keep planning (secondary). It is not a planner question, so there is no asking bar and the composer keeps its ordinary placeholder. Typing something else instead is a new turn, read from scratch, and the confirm stays above it as an unanswered record without its buttons. The buttons show only while the confirm is the latest turn of an open session.
