A proposed card as a planning target

Delta on design/ai-chat/target-picker--search-and-canvas.mock.html and on design-notes.md § The target picker, revised — search that finds a number, and Set as target on the canvas (MOTIR-6895). That section said a proposed card shows no target action. This delta reverses that: a card the plan under review adds can be a target, on the Canvas and the List, at any depth. Only the panels that change are drawn; everything else is the shipped surface.

One name for the thing: the canvas card the person clicks already reads New in its key slot, and the shipped proposed chip (PlanningTargetChip proposedWord) already carries that word on the add card's dashed-accent frame. A proposed target is drawn with that chip everywhere it appears. The word proposed is used where a sentence has to be read aloud or written out: accessible names, the turn's read-out and the rail notes. Never "plan item", never a planItem: id.

From the roadmap, a work item, or a Plans row (?plan=change&planSession=…). The planning overlay opens.

2The plan's pane

The session has a plan under review, so the pane draws PlanProposalViews: List and Canvas. Its add cards read + add and New.

3Select a New card

A click selects it. Its action slot now offers Set as target beside View (panel 2). On the List, the row opens the peek, whose header carries it (panel 3).

4It joins the tray

The composer's tray shows it as a New chip beside any committed targets (panel 4). The next turn sends both.

The second door is unchanged in shape: the composer's search (panel 5) gains a section for this plan's proposals. Neither door exists without a plan under review, because without one there are no proposals.
