The deepen (solid 2 px --el-accent-on-surface outline at offset 2 px on the box, §23.4) is the FRESHER signal: it is news (the fields just landed) and it is solid, while the dotted ring at 7 px is state. On an add card the outline lands on the emphasis ring the shipped card already draws, so what reads is its 600 ms hold and its fade; the orange ring and the breath carry on outside it the whole time, and the outline grows with the box. Neither suppresses the other, and the breath is not restarted by the deepen.

Both triggers draw the same exit: the ring AND chip fade out, the peach wash fades off the card and the box settles back to scale 1, all on var(--transition-duration) with var(--canvas-ease-exit) (Part XXVI §26.8 C2’s tokens), then the cue is removed and the card wears its own op fill again. (a) The step moves on or clears: C2, which Part XXVI specified and the shipped layer never played. (b) The plan leaves generating (planned, approved, declined, or its producer judged dead): the same C2 — this amends §26.8 C10’s “gone in one frame” for the cue only. There is no “cued forever” form.
