/tokens — Font sets

A new section of app/tokens/page.tsx, directly after Typography. It shows every locale's font set: its locales, each role's members with the default marked, and each member's sample in its own face and its set's script. The rest of /tokens is the shipped page and is not drawn here.

1 · Populated — every set, role and member loaded

One card per set, in registry order. A role with one member draws that member alone, with no empty slot for others (most roles here). The mono rows of the CJK sets are a composition rather than a face, and say so.

2 · Not yet on screen — placeholders, no script glyphs

Until the section scrolls into view it renders its heading and one labelled placeholder per set. Every word is Latin: no Han, kana or Hangul character is in the DOM, because one glyph would make the browser fetch that face on arrival.

3 · Loading — fallback face, labelled

Once in view, every set's samples mount at once, each with its own lang, and their faces are requested. Until a face arrives its sample shows in the fallback face with a "loading" badge naming the face, so a fallback is never mistaken for the member.
