Roles & permissions

What each role in motir can do. Workspace owners and admins always hold everything.

These three roles are built into Motir and can’t be changed. Every member of the project can read this page — it’s how the project says what each role means.
Admin
Full control of the project — its settings, its members and all of its work.
8 of 8 role permissions
Built-in
Project2/2
View project Open the project and read its work items, boards, backlog and reports.
Administer project Change project settings — members, workflow, boards, fields, components and automation.
Work items1/1
Edit work items Create, update, assign and move work items, and transition them on the board.
Comments2/2
Add comments Post comments on work items, and edit or delete your own.
Moderate comments Edit and delete anyone’s comments, not only your own.
Attachments2/2
Add attachments Upload files onto work items, and delete the ones you uploaded.
Delete any attachment Remove attachments uploaded by anyone, not only your own.
Watchers1/1
Manage watchers Add and remove other people from a work item’s watcher list.

Does the work: creates and edits work items, comments and attaches files.

1 · The grid at width — the same three cards without the shell. A domain heading carries a held/total count, and each permission’s description collapses to ONE line, so a domain that grows to a dozen rows reads as a scannable list instead of a wall. Withheld permissions stay VISIBLE but recede (muted label, dash mark) — a role’s gaps are part of what it says, and the three columns have to be directly comparable.
