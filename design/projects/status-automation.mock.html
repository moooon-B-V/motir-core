Workflow

The statuses work moves through in Motir, and the moves that are allowed between them. Changes apply to every item in the project.

Status automation

Keep a parent and its children in step. Motir derives status in both directions by default; each switch turns its own direction off.

Roll up parent status from children

A parent follows its children's progress: In Progress once work starts on any child, In Review once the last open child reaches review, and Done once every child is finished. It only ever moves a parent forward, along moves your workflow already allows.

Complete children when a parent is done

When a parent reaches Done — including automatically, when its pull request merges — its unfinished children are completed too. This includes children nobody has started yet, so turn it off if your team tracks child work separately.

Panel 2 · Upward-only — the cascade switched off

The preference the two-switch model exists to express, and the whole argument against one combined toggle: a team that wants the rollup but not the auto-close can have exactly that. An off row keeps its layout and drops only its text tokens to --el-text-faint, so it stays readable rather than disappearing.

Panel 3 · Non-admin — visible, not editable

A member SEES the configuration (the browse-gated read every settings pane uses), with the switches disabled and the save footer replaced by the shipped lock banner. Disabled, never hidden: a reader sees the same setting a sighted admin does, and learns why their items move on their own.
