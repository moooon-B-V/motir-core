The page’s Linked work items strip

Story MOTIR-7567 · design MOTIR-9196. A delta of the page at its address: the strip of hand-linked work items between the title row and the editor, with Link work item, Unlink, paging and every state between. Every panel is drawn in light and in dark.

1Populated — a reader who may link

NEW. The strip sits between the title row and the editor: the page’s hand-linked work items as the product’s live chips, Link work item at the label row’s trailing edge and Unlink on each row. Newest hand link first — the read’s order; the strip never re-sorts.

4Hidden — nothing to show and nobody to show it to

RULE. A live page with no hand links and a reader who may not link: no strip at all — no label, no empty line, no reserved space. The page is exactly the shipped page. One rule chooses among panels 1–4: paint iff the page is live AND (the read served rows OR this reader may link). A loading frame is drawn only for a reader the capability says may link, and an archived page mounts no strip at all (no read is issued for it).

5Loading — the linkable reader

NEW. The label paints with two chip-shaped bars where the chips will sit (aria-hidden) and one polite Loading linked work items…, announced once. No control is drawn while loading: a control must not appear over a list that has not answered. A reader who may not link gets nothing at all — a frame for a strip they may not get is a promise the product then withdraws. It is an in-place skeleton, not a route-level frame: the route’s <Suspense> covers the editor’s lazily loaded code after the gate, and a read inside the surface must not blank the title.

7The row — three renderings of four data states

NEW. Live (accessible: true, not archived): the shipped chip — type icon, mono key, title, status dot — inside a peek link. Archived: the shipped is-archived chip, dashed, with the archive glyph, still navigable — a hand link can predate the archive, so it is drawn as archived and never as unavailable. Unavailable stands for BOTH accessible: false and summary: null: a chip-shaped, non-interactive row reading Unavailable with the tooltip This work item isn’t available to you. — no key, no title, no status — and it keeps its Unlink. The strip cannot use the chip’s bare-key fallback: that exists for a body token, where the author typed the key, and this row’s only data is an id.

8At the page size — Show more

NEW. Fifty chips are served with a cursor; they wrap across lines and the strip ends in Show more. Pressing it appends the next page in place, keeping scroll position and focus, the button busy. No count line — a keyset read knows no total. Fifty per read, capped at 100 (levelPageSize behind lib/pages/workItemPagesCursor.ts); no hand-made row is ever truncated off the list. A failure keeps the rows and shows one alert row at the foot whose Try again re-reads exactly the cursor that failed — never the first page.

11Unlinking — one press, no confirm, no toast

NEW. Unlink takes one press with no confirm step — removing a hand link changes nothing but this list and is one click to remake — and raises no toast: the row’s leaving is the feedback. Busy: the row stays and its Unlink holds a spinner. Success removes the row; on a paged list the rows below keep their order and the cursor is not reset. Failure returns the row to exactly its place with one alert row.
