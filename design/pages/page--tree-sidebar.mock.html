Pages — the page’s sidebar tree and breadcrumb

MOTIR-7367 for Story MOTIR-5753. A DELTA on design/pages/page.mock.html (MOTIR-7271): the page at /pages/{id} gains a sidebar tree with the page selected, and a breadcrumb that replaces “← Pages”. The title, toolbar, editor and save states are the approved base’s and are not redrawn. Spec, copy and keys: design-notes.md § The page tree.

1THE PAGE’S PLACE — the sidebar tree with this page selected and its ancestors expanded, and the breadcrumb that replaces “← Pages”.

Sidebar: a second column between the rail and the page, --el-surface-soft with a right rule, 248px. It is the same PageTree as /pages (MOTIR-7373) in a compact density — 32px rows, 14px per level — with selectedPageId and expandedPath from the trail (MOTIR-7375), so the path to this page is open on arrival and the page itself is the selected row: the rail’s active-row treatment, --el-sidebar-item-bg-active + border + --shadow-subtle, its glyph in --el-icon-active. Its head links back to /pages and holds the hide button. The sidebar is navigation only: no New, no row menu, no drag — arranging happens in /pages.

Breadcrumb: above the title where “← Pages” stood, root-first: Pages › folder… › parent pages… › this page, read from MOTIR-7370’s getPageTrail. It always starts with Pages, so a root page keeps its way back. A folder segment carries the Folder glyph; every segment but the last is a link in --el-text-secondary; the last is the page, --el-text, aria-current="page", not a link. Separators are ChevronRight in --el-icon-muted, aria-hidden.

2THE BREADCRUMB’S GRAMMAR — root page, filed page, and a deep chain truncated in the middle.

Middle truncation: when the chain has more than five segments (Pages and the page included), the breadcrumb keeps Pages, the first segment, the parent and the page and folds everything between into one “…” button (pages.tree.breadcrumb.more as its label). It opens a menu of the hidden segments, root-first, each a link. Each visible segment truncates at 180px (the page at 220px) with an ellipsis and its full text in title. Folders and pages together can reach any depth — folders have no limit, pages stop at 10 (ADR §4) — so truncation is by count, not by kind.

3THE SIDEBAR COLLAPSED — by the reader at a wide width, and by default at a narrow one.

At 1280px and wider the sidebar shows unless the reader hid it; the choice is a per-viewer convenience kept in localStorage (it may come back empty, and then the sidebar shows). Below 1280px it starts hidden — the rail, a 248px tree and a 760px reading column do not fit — and Show page tree opens it as an overlay over the page (--el-overlay-scrim, --shadow-modal), the same component, closed by Esc, by the scrim or by choosing a page. The page stays fully usable with the tree hidden: the breadcrumb is the way up.

4BREADCRUMB → TREE — a folder segment opens /pages with that folder expanded.

A folder has no page of its own, so its segment links to /pages?folder=<folderId>: the tree opens with the path to that folder expanded, the folder scrolled into view and holding the tree’s focus (the focus ring drawn on Specs). A page segment links to that page. The Pages segment and the sidebar’s head link to /pages as it was left.

5THE SIDEBAR’S OWN STATES — loading, failed and long levels, in the tree’s grammar.

The sidebar is PageTree, so it inherits the tree’s row states and strings (panels 5–7 of pages--tree.mock.html) at its compact density. The path to the page is read on the server with the trail, so the first paint already shows the selected row; only levels the reader opens afterwards load lazily. A viewer’s sidebar is identical — it carries no actions for anyone. The page’s not-found and read-only states are unchanged (page.mock.html states 10 and 12): a page that 404s renders no sidebar and no breadcrumb.
