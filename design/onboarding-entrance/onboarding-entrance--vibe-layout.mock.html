4 · How it works, populated — light · 1280 px

Opened in a new tab from the entrance. The hero is the title and body; three section bands follow (say · watch · decide), each with its drawing; then the motir.co link-out (Read it on motir.co → https://motir.co/how-it-works, new tab); then Back to start, which CLOSES this tab and returns the person to the entrance tab, or goes to /onboarding where the tab cannot be closed.

Motir

AI does the work. You make the calls.

You bring the idea. Motir asks until it understands, then makes the plan, the design, the product and the documents step by step, out in the open, and stops wherever a choice is yours.

Say what you want

A few sentences or a messy brain dump. Motir asks until it understands.

Watch it take shape

The plan, the designs, the product and the documents get made step by step, out in the open.

You decide

Every piece comes back to you to look at, try and approve. You stay in charge of what ships.

How Motir works, for developers

Every Motir project runs through the same instrument: the modules are Motir and its agents, and the switches are yours. motir.co walks through it module by module.
