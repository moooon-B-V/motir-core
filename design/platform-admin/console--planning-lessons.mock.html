Split a subtask that touches two repositories

Regular planning Acme Corp · platform / web-app Injected
Injected Edit Promote
Make globalEvery organisation’s planner reads it. Its type stays Regular planning.
Make global planning craftEvery planner reads it, during onboarding and regular planning alike.
Why it matters

A subtask that changes two repositories cannot ship as one pull request, so it stalls at review and its dependants wait on it.

How to apply

When a subtask names files in more than one repository, split it into one subtask per repository and link them with a dependency in the order they must merge.

What happened

The planner proposed one subtask spanning motir-core and motir-ai; it was rejected at review and re-planned as two.
