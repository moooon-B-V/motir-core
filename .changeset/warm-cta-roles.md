New role tokens for a warm primary button (MOTIR-8251 · MOTIR-8254). `--el-cta-warm` is the palette's decorative highlight used as a button fill, and `--el-cta-warm-text` its label: the body ink in light and the page ink in dark, because white on the motir orange measures 3.8:1 light and 3.3:1 dark while the dark labels measure 4.65:1 and 5.9:1. A consumer applies them as a treatment of `Button`'s primary variant (`bg-(--el-cta-warm) text-(--el-cta-warm-text)`). No existing token moves.
