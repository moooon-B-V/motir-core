New role tokens for the Motir mark at work (MOTIR-9212 · MOTIR-9213). `--el-mark-wave-1..3` are the three hues the mark cycles through while it flows on the ink circle (`--el-accent`): each wave hue (`--el-showcase-wave-1..3`) mixed toward the ink on the circle (`--el-accent-text`), 35% hue in the base and 60% in the Motir palette, light and dark. The raw wave hues fall to 1.00:1 on the circle in the palettes whose accent is a wave hue; these keep 3:1 (WCAG 1.4.11) for each role, every blend between two neighbours and every point of the ramp toward the ink, in every palette and theme. No existing token moves.
